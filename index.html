Dhama

A personal automation tool that publishes and manages video content on its operator's own YouTube channel.

What it does

Dhama is a small, self-hosted automation project. It connects to YouTube through the official YouTube Data API v3 and performs routine channel tasks on behalf of the person running it:

  • Uploading video files to the operator's own channel
  • Setting and updating video titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails
  • Managing playlists and video visibility

Who it is for

Dhama is not a public product. It is operated by a single person for their own channel, and is not offered as a service to third parties.

Google API access

Dhama requests the following OAuth scopes, and nothing else:

  • .../auth/youtube.upload — upload videos
  • .../auth/youtube.force-ssl — manage videos, playlists and captions over HTTPS

Access is granted by the operator signing in with their own Google Account. Dhama's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
